Social Runners

All levels welcome. This is a social running group — we focus on chatting, meeting people, and enjoying the run rather than performance. We speak both Spanish and English. Slow-to-medium pace (about 5 km) — perfect for beginners and anyone who prefers a relaxed run.

About the community

Social Runners is for people who like the idea of running together at a relaxed pace, meeting new people, and keeping things low-pressure.

Who it’s for

Runners who enjoy easy-paced runs, are happy to chat between intervals, and want a welcoming group rather than a competitive club. New runners are very welcome.

What to expect

Regular meetups, simple routes, no pressure on pace, and a focus on consistency and community.

Join a group run

We schedule all our runs through Meetup. Check the event list, pick a time that works, and RSVP so we know you’re coming.

How to join

1. Visit our Meetup page
2. Join the Valencia Social Runners group
3. RSVP to an upcoming event and meet us at the starting point

First 5K

1:1 beginner coaching focused on building a consistent running habit, starting safely, and working toward your first 5K.

Who this is for

You’re new to running (or returning after a break), want to avoid common beginner injuries, and prefer clear guidance instead of guessing your way through training.

Coaching goal

The main goal is to help you develop a sustainable running habit and start running safely, with a long-term vision of running your first 5K. The exact time it takes to reach 5K comfortably will depend on your current fitness, injury history, and how much you can realistically commit to training.

How 1:1 coaching works

You’ll work 1:1 with a UESCA-certified running coach. We start with a short conversation about your goals, background, and schedule, then build a realistic plan that fits your life and training history.

What’s included

• Beginner-friendly, personalised plan focused on habit and safety
• Gradual progression using easy runs and walk–run intervals
• Weekly 1:1 check-ins (message or call) to review how you’re feeling and adjust the plan
• Practical tips on pacing, warm-ups, and recovery so you feel confident starting out

How to get started

If you’re interested in First 5K 1:1 coaching, please contact our group organizer through the Valencia Social Runners page on Meetup and mention you’re asking about the First 5K coaching option.
